# 📊 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an **interactive sales performance dashboard for Adidas**, built using **Power BI**.  
The dashboard focuses on answering **real business questions** related to sales trends, profitability, regional performance, product contribution, and retailer analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
The dashboard is designed to:
- Track overall **sales, profit, units sold, and margins**
- Identify **monthly sales trends**
- Analyze **regional and state-wise performance**
- Understand **top-performing products and retailers**
- Support **data-driven decision-making** for sales and marketing strategies

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals & Insights
### KPI Cards
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Average Price per Unit
- Average Margin

### Charts & Visuals
- **Line Chart:** Total Sales by Month (trend analysis)
- **Map:** Total Sales by State (geographic performance)
- **Bar Chart:** Total Sales by Product Category
- **Donut Chart:** Regional Sales Contribution
- **Stacked Column Chart:** Total Sales by Retailer

### Slicers
- Region
- Invoice Date Range

These allow users to interactively explore the data.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- The **West region** contributes the highest share of total sales
- Certain retailers consistently outperform others in revenue contribution
- Sales show **seasonal trends** with noticeable monthly peaks
- Product categories vary significantly in profitability and volume
